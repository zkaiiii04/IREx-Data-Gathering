# IREx Research Data Gathering

How the Institute of Rehab Excellence collects its staff's research activity to track department KPIs, which are reviewed at the bi-annual department meetings.

## Organisation

**IREx**:
The Institute of Rehab Excellence, the department whose research activity is tracked.
_Avoid_: the institute, the department (when IREx is meant)

**IREx Member**:
A staff member of IREx who is expected to report their research activity.
_Avoid_: staff, researcher, user

**KPI Review**:
The bi-annual department meeting where IREx's research KPIs are reported.
_Avoid_: bi-annual report, department meeting

## Research activity

**Research Output**:
A one-off piece of research activity with a date: a Publication, a Poster Presentation or a Media Appearance. Each one is recorded once, however many IREx Members contributed.
_Avoid_: submission, entry, output (alone)

**Publication**:
A Research Output published in a journal, book or similar venue.
_Avoid_: paper, article

**Poster Presentation**:
A Research Output presented as a poster at a conference or event.
_Avoid_: poster, presentation

**Media Appearance**:
A Research Output in which IREx research or an IREx Member appears in the media.
_Avoid_: media, press

**Research Project**:
A piece of research that runs over time, with its own funding, status and Principal Investigator. It is tracked as its own KPI and is not a Research Output.
_Avoid_: study, grant

**Principal Investigator (PI)**:
The IREx Member who leads a Research Project.
_Avoid_: lead, owner
