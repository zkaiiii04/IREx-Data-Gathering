# IREx Research Data Gathering

How the Institute of Rehab Excellence collects its staff's research activity to track department KPIs, which are reviewed at the bi-annual department meetings.

## Organisation

**IREx**:
The Institute of Rehab Excellence, the department whose research activity is tracked.
_Avoid_: the institute, the department (when IREx is meant)

**IREx Member**:
A staff member of IREx who is expected to report their research activity.
_Avoid_: staff, researcher, user

**Family Group**:
An IREx Member's professional group: Rehab Med, Rehab AHS, Rehab Nursing or MSW.
_Avoid_: profession, department

**IREx Admin**:
The IREx staff who keep the main database, record activity statuses and prepare the KPI Review.
_Avoid_: collector, admin team

**KPI Review**:
The bi-annual department meeting where IREx's research KPIs are reported.
_Avoid_: bi-annual report, department meeting

## Research activity

**Research Output**:
A one-off piece of research activity with a date: a Publication, a Presentation or a Media Appearance. Each one is recorded once, however many IREx Members contributed.
_Avoid_: submission, entry, output (alone)

**Publication**:
A Research Output published in a journal, book or similar venue. It moves through Under Review, Accepted and Published.
_Avoid_: paper, article

**IREx Author**:
An IREx Member listed among a Publication's authors, in any position. A Publication counts as IREx's when it has at least one IREx Author.
_Avoid_: TTSH Contributor, lead author

**Presentation**:
A Research Output presented at a conference, symposium or seminar, either as a poster or orally. It moves from Accepted to Presented.
_Avoid_: poster (alone), talk, abstract

**Media Appearance**:
A Research Output in which IREx research or an IREx Member appears in one media outlet. A story carried by several outlets is several Media Appearances.
_Avoid_: media, press

**Talent Development**:
An award or personal grant given to an IREx Member: either a Talent Development grant, which has an LOA and an amount, or a Special Award given in recognition.
_Avoid_: award (alone), accolade

**Education Activity**:
A course, workshop, webinar or sharing session that IREx Members run as trainers or speakers, for professionals or for patients, caregivers and the public.
_Avoid_: training, outreach, education (alone)

**Research Project**:
A piece of research that runs over time, with its own funding, status and Principal Investigator. It is tracked as its own KPI and is not a Research Output. Its category is Research, Innovation or Lien Foundation (LF).
_Avoid_: study, grant

**Oversight Project**:
A Research Project that IREx oversees but does not run. Its statuses carry the suffix "(Oversight)".
_Avoid_: supported project

**Principal Investigator (PI)**:
The IREx Member who leads a Research Project.
_Avoid_: lead, owner

**Letter of Award (LOA)**:
The funder's award for a Research Project or Talent Development grant. Its start and end dates bound the funded period.
_Avoid_: award letter, grant period
