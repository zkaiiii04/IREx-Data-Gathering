# IREx Research Data Gathering

How the Institute of Rehab Excellence collects the research activity of Rehab Staff to track its KPIs, which are reviewed at the KPI Review about twice a year.

## Organisation

**IREx**:
The Institute of Rehab Excellence, which tracks the research activity of Rehab Staff.
_Avoid_: the institute, the department (when IREx is meant)

**IREx Committee**:
IREx's main committee. Its members are *IREx Committee Members*.
_Avoid_: IREx Member (for anyone not on the committee)

**Rehab Staff**:
Anyone working in rehab at TTSH: rehab doctors, rehab nurses and rehab allied health staff (therapists, MSWs and others). Rehab Staff report research activity, and their activity is what IREx tracks.
_Avoid_: IREx Member, clinicians, staff (alone)

**Family Group**:
A member of Rehab Staff's professional group: Rehab Med, Rehab AHS, Rehab Nursing or MSW.
_Avoid_: profession, department

**IREx Admin**:
The IREx staff who keep the main database, record activity statuses and prepare the KPI Review.
_Avoid_: collector, admin team

**KPI Review**:
The IREx Committee meeting where IREx's research KPIs are reported. It's held about twice a year, on dates that vary by a few months.
_Avoid_: committee meeting, bi-annual report, department meeting

**Main Database**:
IREx Admin's workbook of every Research Output, Talent Development, Education Activity and Project. It's the source of truth, edited by hand.
_Avoid_: database (alone), master file

**Collection Workbook**:
The shared workbook that Rehab Staff check their existing records in and add new ones to. It's made fresh from the Main Database for one calendar year. Rehab Staff never see the Main Database itself.
_Avoid_: staff input workbook, staff workbook, staff copy, submission excel

**Submission**:
One completed response to the reporting form. A single Submission may report several Research Outputs, Talent Developments, Education Activities and Projects.
_Avoid_: entry, response, form

**Submitter**:
The person who completes a Submission. The form is sent to Rehab Staff, so the Submitter is the member of Rehab Staff the reported items belong to.
_Avoid_: reporter, respondent, user

## Research activity

**Research Output**:
A one-off piece of research activity with a date: a Publication, a Presentation or a Media Appearance. Each one is recorded once, however many Rehab Staff contributed. It is not a Project.
_Avoid_: submission, entry, output (alone)

**Publication**:
A Research Output published in a journal, book or similar venue. It moves through Under Review, Accepted and Published.
_Avoid_: paper, article

**Rehab Author**:
A member of Rehab Staff listed among a Publication's authors, in any position. A Publication counts when it has at least one Rehab Author.
_Avoid_: IREx Author, TTSH Contributor, lead author

**Presentation**:
A Research Output presented at a conference, symposium or seminar, either as a poster or orally. It moves from Accepted to Presented.
_Avoid_: poster (alone), talk, abstract

**Media Appearance**:
A Research Output in which rehab research or a member of Rehab Staff appears in one media outlet. A story carried by several outlets is several Media Appearances.
_Avoid_: media, press

**Talent Development**:
An award or personal grant given to a member of Rehab Staff: either a Talent Development grant, which has an LOA and an amount, or a Special Award given in recognition.
_Avoid_: award (alone), accolade

**Education Activity**:
A course, workshop, webinar or sharing session that Rehab Staff run as trainers, speakers or organisers, for professionals or for patients, caregivers and the public.
_Avoid_: training, outreach, education (alone)

**Project**:
A piece of research or innovation work that runs over time, with its own funding, status and Principal Investigator. It is tracked as its own KPI. Its category is Research, Innovation or Lien Foundation (LF).
_Avoid_: Research Project, study, grant

**Oversight Project**:
A Project that IREx oversees but does not run. Its statuses carry the suffix "(Oversight)".
_Avoid_: supported project

**Principal Investigator (PI)**:
The person who leads a Project.
_Avoid_: lead, owner

**Letter of Award (LOA)**:
The funder's award for a Project or Talent Development grant. Its start and end dates bound the funded period.
_Avoid_: award letter, grant period
